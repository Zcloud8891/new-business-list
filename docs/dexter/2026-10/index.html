Questions

What exactly do I get?

A Google Sheet with every Lane County business that registered with the Oregon Secretary of State in October 2026: business name, entity type, filing date, street address, city and ZIP, registered agent, and the owners or officers named in the filing, plus a category guess. Right after checkout you're sent straight to the Sheet.

How often does it update?

Monthly. The state posts last month's filings somewhere between the 5th and the 17th of each month. A script checks every day, and a new tab for the new month shows up on its own, usually the same day the state posts it.

How much is it?

$9 a month. Cancel anytime.

Are phone numbers and emails included?

No. The state filing doesn't include them, and we don't make them up. You get what's on the public record: names, addresses and filing details.

Where does the data come from?

The Oregon Secretary of State's public business registry, published on data.oregon.gov as "New Businesses Registered Last Month." Oregon law puts this data in the public domain (ORS 276A.368). We clean it, sort it by Lane County ZIP code, and add categories.

Is it complete and accurate?

It's as good as the state's file, and the state doesn't guarantee it, so neither can we. The list is provided as-is. Categories are a guess from the business name. A few businesses only list a mailing address.

Why no towing companies?

We leave towing and roadside-assistance businesses out of every list on purpose.

A reminder before you reach out

Many new owners are sole proprietors using a home address and a personal cell phone. If you email them, follow CAN-SPAM: honest subject lines, your postal address, and an opt-out you honor within 10 business days. If you call or text, the TCPA bans autodialed, prerecorded or AI-voice marketing to cell phones without prior written consent, and the Do Not Call Registry can apply. Some owners have asked the state not to disclose a home address under ORS 192.445. Please respect that. Mail and an honest, useful offer work best anyway.
